The House That Never Had a Door

About places we remember, even after we have left them behind.

There are places we leave without really leaving them behind.

Sometimes it is a house. Sometimes it is a street, a room, a chair beside a window, or a corner where we once waited for someone who never came.

We remember these places not because they were extraordinary, but because something inside us happened there.

Some places remain

Years can pass and the walls may change. The windows may be replaced. Someone else may live there.

Yet memory has a strange way of keeping certain places exactly as they were.

In our minds, the afternoon light still enters through the same window. The floor still makes the same sound. The silence still feels familiar.

Perhaps we don't remember places. Perhaps we remember who we were there.

That may be why some places feel strangely close even when they are far away.

We are not always longing for the place itself. We are longing for a version of ourselves that existed there.

The door that was never there

Maybe that is why this house never needed a door.

It was never meant to keep us inside. It simply gave us somewhere to remember.

And perhaps that is enough.
